Research

CO2 reduction on greigite (Fe3S4)

Greigite is an iron sulfide mineral with structural similarities to the active sites of some enzymes, and it has been proposed as a prebiotic catalyst. I simulate the electrochemical reduction of CO2 at the greigite–water interface with explicit solvent, following reaction mechanisms and free-energy barriers under applied potential.

Ab initio molecular dynamics, CP2K, free-energy sampling
