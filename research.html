Research

CO2 reduction on iron-sulfur minerals

Greigite (Fe3S4), Mackinawite (FeS), and other iron-sulfur minerals bear a striking resemblance to the catalytic iron-sulfur cluster hearts of several highly conserved enzymes at the core of metabolism, such as ferredoxin and carbon monoxide dehydrogenase. I use ab initio molecular dynamics combined with enhanced sampling methods to simulate the electrochemical reduction of CO2 at the mineral–water interface with explicit solvent to calculate reaction free energies and rates.

Ab initio molecular dynamics, CP2K, enhanced sampling, free-energy calculations

Hydrodynamics for whole-cell models

I am exploring ways to incorporate explicit hydrodynamic effects into the whole-cell simulation platform MEDYAN. This includes a GPU accelerated implementation of the stochastic immersed boundary bethod, as well of spectral, slender body theory-based methods.

Julia, CUDA, spectral methods

Cytoneme mediated cell-to-cell communication tissue models

In collaboration with an experimental biology group, I am creating a model of fruit fly cell communication, mediated by specialized filopodia called cytonemes. The model consists of simple mechanical model of cytoneme tips represented as Brownian random walkers, coupled with a stochastic jump processes model of an internal cellular gene network.

Julia, differential equations, stochastic processes
