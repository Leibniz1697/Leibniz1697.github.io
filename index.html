I use simulations to watch CO2 turn into fuel on the surface of an iron sulfide mineral.

I'm a Ph.D. student in Chemical Physics at the University of Maryland, College Park, working where electrochemistry, surface science and first-principles simulation meet. I use ab initio molecular dynamics to understand how reactions happen at solid–liquid interfaces, with a focus on earth-abundant catalysts for CO2 conversion.

Before this, I earned an M.S. in Physics at California State University, Fresno, and a B.A. at St. John’s College. Outside research, I enjoy [a hobby or two].
