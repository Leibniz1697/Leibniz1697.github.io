I use simulations to watch CO2 turn into fuel on the surface of an iron sulfide mineral.

I'm a Ph.D. student in Chemical Physics at the University of Maryland, College Park. My research involves modeling eukaryotic cells, communication between cells, and the origin of life. Use the Julia to write custom biophysics and quantum codes, and established electronic structure and molecular dynamics packages like CP2K, Lammps, and Gromacs.

Before entering the Ph.D., I earned an M.S. in Physics from California State University, Fresno, and a B.A. from St. John’s College, Annapolis. Outside research, I like playing baroque music on keyboard and the viola da gamba.

More about my work is on the Research page.
